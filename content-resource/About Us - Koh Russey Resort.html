What you need to know about Koh Russey

About our superb resort

Just a short speedboat ride from the Khmer Riviera, Koh Russey Resort is nestled in the heart of Koh Russey, aka Bamboo Island. Our resort features 50 luxurious pavilions and 13 private pool villas, set on a 1.2km private beach with stunning ocean views.

Check Out our villas

Our Concept

Our design honors local artisans by incorporating traditional craftsmanship and materials. This seamless fusion of architecture, landscaping, and interior design creates a unique and authentic experience.

At One with Nature

Occupying 25 hectares between the western cape and two kilometers of pristine beach, Koh Russey Resort offers breathtaking views and complete privacy. Situated in the Koh Rong archipelago, our resort is surrounded by pristine bays and dramatic headlands, perfectly blending with nature.
