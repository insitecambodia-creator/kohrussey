At Koh Russey Private Island Resort, we create more than just ceremonies — we craft unforgettable love stories. Tucked away on Cambodia’s tranquil Bamboo Island, our resort offers couples the ultimate blend of privacy, natural beauty, and personalized service. From an intimate engagement or proposal on a copper-sand beach to a glamorous private island wedding or a serene honeymoon in a beachfront villa, every moment is designed around your dreams.

What sets us apart is the balance of exclusive luxury and authentic Cambodian charm. Picture saying “yes” as the sun sets over the Gulf of Thailand, capturing timeless memories with a pre-wedding photoshoot against the gorgeous shores, or retreating to your own tailor-made honeymoon package with spa rituals, sunset dining, and island adventures.

Whether you imagine a grand beachfront celebration or a romantic escape for two, our team ensures that every detail reflects your unique love story. At Koh Russey, you create a once-in-a-lifetime experience in one of the most sought-after beach resorts in Cambodia.
