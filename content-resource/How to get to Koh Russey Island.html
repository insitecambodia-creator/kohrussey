How to get here

Koh Russey, aka Bamboo Island, is accessible by sea or air. 

Our Ream Lounge

Your journey begins at Ream Lounge, our pier on the mainland. 

Here, our receptionist will greet you with refreshments and hot or cold towels while our crew handles your luggage. The air-conditioned lounge offers comfortable seating, tables, and restrooms.
