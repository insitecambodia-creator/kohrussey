Why Cambodia’s “Green Season” Might Be the Best Time to Travel

For years, travelers have been told to avoid Cambodia during the rainy months. But today, the narrative is changing. The Ministry of Tourism has officially embraced a new term: the “Green Season.” And honestly, it couldn’t be more accurate.

Because what many still call the “low season” or “monsoon season” is, in reality, one of the most beautiful, and underrated, times to experience the country. Especially if you’re dreaming of a peaceful resort in Cambodia or a quiet escape to one of the country’s stunning islands.

A Different Kind of Rhythm

Let’s start with the biggest misconception: “It rains all day.”

In truth, Cambodia’s Green Season follows a surprisingly gentle rhythm. Rain typically falls once a day, often in short, refreshing bursts. Depending on the time of year, showers might come in the late afternoon, evening, or early morning, leaving plenty of time for sunshine in between.
For travelers, this means you can still enjoy the beach, explore nature, or relax by the pool, just with a dramatic sky and cooler air.

Cambodia at Its Most Beautiful

There’s a reason it’s called the Green Season.

As the rains return, Cambodia transforms. Landscapes that may feel dry during the hot months suddenly come alive. Rice fields glow in vibrant shades of green, forests thicken, and the air feels fresher, softer, more alive.

If you’re staying at a beach resort in Cambodia, the contrast is even more striking, lush tropical gardens meeting calm, grey-blue seas under ever-changing skies. It’s a photographer’s dream and a nature lover’s paradise.

Fewer Crowds, More Space

One of the greatest advantages of traveling during the Green Season? Space.

Popular destinations like Koh Rong or Koh Rong Samloem become quieter, more intimate. Beaches feel more private. Restaurants are calmer. Service is often more attentive.

For those seeking a more exclusive experience, this is the perfect time to enjoy a Koh Rong resort or a private island setting without the high-season crowds.

A More Relaxed Island Experience

The islands of Cambodia take on a completely different atmosphere during the Green Season. Life slows down. The pace becomes gentler.

It’s the ideal time for:

  • Long, uninterrupted beach walks
  • Spa treatments with the sound of rain in the background
  • Reading by the pool as clouds roll in
  • Quiet dinners overlooking the sea

For couples, honeymooners, or anyone seeking a break from busy routines, this season offers something rare: true tranquility.

Better Value, Same Beauty

Another reason savvy travelers love the Green Season is the value.

With fewer visitors, many resorts in Cambodia offer more attractive rates, special packages, and added flexibility. You’re essentially enjoying the same stunning destination, but with more space and often at a better price.

When Rain Becomes Part of the Experience

Instead of seeing rain as a disruption, many travelers come to appreciate it as part of the journey.

A tropical shower can turn an ordinary afternoon into a memorable moment, whether you’re watching it from your villa terrace, enjoying a cocktail as the sky darkens, or falling asleep to the calming sound of rain.

It adds texture, atmosphere, and a sense of place that you simply don’t get during the dry season.

Discover Cambodia Differently

The Green Season isn’t about compromise, it’s about perspective.

It’s about seeing Cambodia at its most alive. Feeling the freshness of the air. Enjoying destinations without the crowds. And discovering a slower, more authentic rhythm of travel.

So if you’ve been thinking about visiting a resort in Cambodia or escaping to one of the country’s beautiful islands, don’t overlook this time of year.

You might just find it’s the best decision you make.
