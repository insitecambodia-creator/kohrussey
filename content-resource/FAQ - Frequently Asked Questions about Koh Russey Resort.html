Frequently Asked Questions

General Questions

What is Koh Russey Resort?

Koh Russey Resort is a luxurious beachfront destination located on Koh Russey Island in Cambodia. We offer a serene escape with stunning villas, exceptional dining, and a range of activities designed for relaxation and adventure.

What does "Koh Russey" mean?

Koh is the phonetic transcription of a Khmer word for island. as you will see in Koh Rong, Koh Kong or the famous Koh Lanta. Thailand will also share is similar sounding word (ko) as in Ko Samui. Russey simply means bamboo, so one would suspect that this small island was well known for its bamboo plantation.

Is Koh Russey Resort affiliated with Alila Villa?

No, Koh Russey Resort is no longer affiliated with the Alila Villa brand. We have transitioned to operate independently, providing our guests with a unique experience tailored to our vision of luxury and hospitality.

Are there other resorts on Bamboo Island?

Yes, Bamboo Island is home to two resorts, including Jati Resort, which offers its own unique style and services. While Koh Russey Resort provides a distinctive experience with luxurious accommodations and a serene environment tailored to relaxation and privacy, Jati Resort caters to a different clientele with its particular charm. Each resort on the island has its own appeal, giving visitors a range of options to explore during their stay.

Is Koh Russey on Koh Rong?

Koh Rong is an island, and is most likely becoming one of Cambodia’s most notorious island; yet, Koh Rong is also an archipelago, which comprises Koh Rong Samlem and Koh Russey. So we are not on the Koh Rong Island, but we do belong to the Koh Rong Archipelago…  

What is the closest Airport?

We are about 30 minutes drive from the Sihanoukville International Airport, The airport code is KOS (as in “Kompong Saom” the alternate and former name for Sihanoukville) 

Accommodation

What types of accommodations are available?

We offer two (2) main types of accommodation: the Pavilions and the Villas. Pavilions are rooms either on the ground floor or first floor, facing the garden or the ocean. On the other hand villas is a more private option, each coming with its own private swimming pool. Villas come is three different sizes: 1, 2 or 4 bedrooms.

Amenities and Services

What amenities does the resort offer?

Koh Russey Resort features a range of amenities including:

  • Infinity pool
  • Spa and wellness center
  • On-site dining options
  • Water sports activities
  • Fitness center
  • Complimentary Wi-Fi

Is there a shop or minimart on the island?

At this time, we do not boast any mart or shop. However, you can acquire a few items and souvenirs from our reception. But we do not have any food products (Noodles, Snacks, etc…).
Also, for safety reason only, we do not allow outside food on the premises.

Are there dining options available at the resort?

Yes! At the moment, we have two dining venues offering a variety of cuisines, from local Cambodian dishes to international favorites. Our chefs use fresh, locally sourced ingredients to create memorable dining experiences.

Restaurants

Does the hotel provide laundry service?

Yes! We sure do! In every room and villa, you will find a laundry bag and a simple form to list your items. In normal situation, we will give you your clean clothes the next day, about 24h. We cannot offer express service at this time.

Can we fly a personal drone over the island?

The first answer is yes, you may fly your drone over the island to film or take pictures of our beautiful setting. However, we will kindly ask you to respect everyone’s privacy by not flying and filming over private villas with guests.   

Also, you will need to limit your flights over our resort and our side of the island. The Jati Resort is off limit.

Others

Do you provide transportation or shuttle to Koh Rong?

This is something we had done in the past and we can reproduce. Our speed boat can take you and your party (a maximum of 20 guests) to Koh Rong – or Koh Rong Salem for a fee of $990. It should take less than 40 minutes to reach the island.

Alternatively, we can drop you at the Ream Station (18 min), then you take a taxi to the Koh Rong Pier (35 – 55 minutes depending on traffic), then a 30 minutes speed boat to the island.
