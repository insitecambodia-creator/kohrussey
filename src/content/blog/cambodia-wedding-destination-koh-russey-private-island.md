## Private-Island Wedding Venue in Cambodia

Located off the coast of southern Cambodia, Koh Russey — also known as Bamboo Island — is a haven of peace and natural beauty. This private island resort is renowned for its powdery white sand beaches, lush tropical gardens, and sleek architecture that blends seamlessly with the landscape. As a luxury resort in Cambodia, Koh Russey delivers more than just scenery — it offers complete exclusivity, elegance, and soul-stirring tranquility. It’s the kind of place where every moment feels cinematic, and every backdrop is photo-worthy.
